Pushkar Ghanekar

scientist · builder · writer

I use science, data, and AI to design better medicines at Eli Lilly. I lead Frontier AI, a group building scientific AI platforms to accelerate therapeutic discovery.

I earned my Ph.D. at Purdue University, where I studied atomistic chemistry in energy-storage and conversion systems. I am interested in how medicines are discovered, developed, and commercialized—and in the tools that make that work more creative.

Outside work, I enjoy cooking, gaming, history, philosophy, and longer-than-necessary bike rides. I also keep a data science blog for notes on life science, algorithms, and code.

Writing helps me work out what I think. Some of those thoughts live here.
