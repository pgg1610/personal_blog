Pushkar Ghanekar

scientist · builder · epicure

Welcome to my personal website. It is good to have you! I am passionate about science and technology to improve our lives.

In my day-to-day I use science, data, and AI systems to design better medicine at Lilly. I lead Frontier AI at Lilly, a boutique group I built from scratch that works at the intersection of lab automation, AI agents, and molecule discovery.

I earned my Ph.D. at Purdue University with Prof. Jeffrey Greeley, where I studied atomistic simulations and how to model energy storage and conversion systems. I am interested in how medicines are discovered, developed, and commercialized—and in the tools that make that work more creative.

Outside work, I enjoy cooking, gaming, history, philosophy, and longer-than-necessary bike rides. I also keep a data science blog for notes on life science, algorithms, and code.

Writing helps me work out what I think. Some of those thoughts live here.
